Privacy Policy

Exit Order · com.hakaru.exitorder · Last updated 23 September 2026

Exit Order collects nothing. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no network access at all. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information. The app has no sign-in, no profile, no email capture and no contact form. It does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere even in principle.

What is stored on your device

So that the game can remember where you were, it saves the following in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • Which levels you have finished, and the stars you earned
  • The level currently in progress, so closing the app loses nothing
  • Your hearts and when they last refilled
  • Your settings — sound, haptics, grid lines, theme

This never leaves the device. It is not backed up to us, because there is no "us" to back it up to. Other apps cannot read it.

Permissions

The app requests one permission: VIBRATE, used for the small haptic tap when an arrow leaves the board or a move is refused. You can turn haptics off in Settings. No camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or network permissions are requested.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. No advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no tracking identifiers, no advertising ID.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is safe for children: since it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. It contains no ads, no purchases and no way to communicate with other players.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Reset progress in the app to erase every level, star and setting immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all of it as well. Because nothing is ever sent off the device, there is nothing for you to request from us.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
