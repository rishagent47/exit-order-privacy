Privacy Policy

Exit Order · com.hakaru.exitorder · Last updated 4 November 2026

Exit Order collects as little as it can. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts of ours. The game works fully offline. Two optional features use Google services, and only if you switch them on: notifications and cloud save.

Information we collect

We never ask for your name, email address, location or contacts. The only things that leave your phone are these, and only for the feature you chose:

  • A notification token (a device identifier). If you allow notifications, the game registers with Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your installation a random token so that messages can reach it. The game joins up to three topics with it: essential notices, reminders and news, following your choices in Settings. The token is not linked to your name or account. Until you allow notifications, no token is created and nothing is sent.
  • Your game progress, in your own Google account. If you turn on Settings → Back up to Google Play Games, the game saves your finished levels, stars, streak, medals and League standing to Google Play Games Saved Games, in your own Google account, so you can restore them on another phone. Google stores it on your behalf; we cannot see it. Signing in to Play Games also tells the game your Play Games display name, which it shows in Settings and keeps on the phone.

We do not sell, rent or share any of this with anyone else.

Reminders on your phone

Daily reminders (a streak about to break, today's puzzle) are worked out and scheduled on your phone from your own progress. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

What is stored on your device

So that the game can remember where you were, it saves the following in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • Which levels you have finished, and the stars you earned
  • The level currently in progress, so closing the app loses nothing
  • Your hearts, streak, medals and League standing
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, theme, notifications, cloud save

Other apps cannot read it.

Services we use

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), for notifications. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Play Games Services (Google), for cloud save. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit.

Permissions

  • VIBRATE: the small haptic tap when an arrow leaves the board or a move is refused. Haptics can be turned off in Settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13 and newer): to show notifications. Android asks you first, and you can say no.
  • INTERNET and network state: for notifications and cloud save only. The game itself plays offline.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: so reminders already scheduled still arrive after your phone restarts.

No camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions are requested.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. No advertising SDK, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising ID.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It has no ads, no purchases and no way to talk to other players. The optional features above collect no information that identifies a child.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Notifications: turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in Android's settings. The game then leaves its topics.
  • Cloud save: turn off Back up to Google Play Games in Settings. To delete the saved copy, remove Exit Order's data in the Google Play Games app or from your Google account.
  • Everything on the phone: Settings → Reset progress erases every level, star and streak. Uninstalling removes all of it, the notification token included.
  • You can also email us and we will help with any request.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting anything new, this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
